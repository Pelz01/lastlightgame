## 05:25 WAT — reproducible NPC-on/off strategy matrix

- Added `scripts/compare_strategies.py` and three regressions for six deterministic complete-match scenarios: dependable supply, one active supplier, and total player withholding, each with the same initial state and player orders under NPC-off and NPC-on conditions.
- The runner records full round traces, survival/completion, conditional winner indicators, merchant profit, rejected actions, stranded merchant-rounds, and final colony state; repeated runs must be byte-for-byte equivalent as Python data.
- Dependable supply survived 11 rounds without the NPC and completed 20 with it; the single-supplier scenario survived 8 rounds without the NPC and completed 20 with it. Total withholding collapsed at round 5 without the NPC and round 11 with it, proving the rescue policy delays but does not silently erase complete player defection.
- All six simulations had zero rejected actions and zero stranded merchant-rounds. These are deterministic AI-policy simulations, not human playtests or evidence of balance. Next: add genuinely competing strategies (emergency hoarding, fuel specialization, mixed production, opportunism) and quantify freerider subsidy before considering resale.
