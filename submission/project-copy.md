# Project copy — draft for review

## One-line pitch

Get rich. Keep the lights on. Trade supplies across a fragile space colony, compete with other merchants, and keep everyone alive.

## Form description

Last Light is a 20-round trading strategy game where two people and a computer merchant compete for profit while keeping a space colony alive. Players buy oxygen, medicine and fuel, move cargo rovers between buildings, and make deliveries. They can also offer cargo at their own prices and buy another merchant's whole lot. A shortage can end the match for everyone, including the richest player.

NEAR testnet records merchant ownership and enforces credits, cargo, market offers and round results. Tess, the computer merchant, uses a separate NEAR wallet with permission limited to submitting her own moves. She can buy player offers, trade and deliver supplies under the same cargo and travel limits. The board shows rovers, routes and colony reactions; solo practice needs no wallet. Online matches can be recovered with the same wallet and match code.

A complete two-browser, 20-round testnet match, twenty signed NPC orders and recovery after a backend restart are recorded in the repository. Credits and cargo are game units with no cash value. Human enjoyment and economic balance still need playtesting.

## Why this track

Proposed track: **Games With Real Economies** (`games`). The demonstration centers on the delivery loop, player-priced trades, finite shared supplies and an independently signing NPC. It does not depend on a speculative token price. Social login, NEP-245 items and mainnet deployment are not claimed.
