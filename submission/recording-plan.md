# Demo recording plan — 60–90 seconds

Use a desktop recording for desktop footage. Record the phone layout separately if including it. Keep the game controls readable; remove notifications and unrelated tabs. No intro animation or architecture slides are needed.

| Time | Show | Suggested narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 s | Landing page, then Play solo and the short introduction | “In Last Light, getting rich is only useful if the colony survives.” |
| 8–23 s | Load 3 oxygen and 2 tank fuel; confirm; point to 17 credits and loaded cargo | “Buy supplies at the Depot. Cargo space and fuel limit what you can carry.” |
| 23–36 s | Travel to Habitat; let the rover arrive | “Choose a destination. Travel uses fuel and takes a separate move.” |
| 36–50 s | Sell the oxygen; show increased credits, supply change and Delivery complete | “A delivery earns credits and keeps the Habitat supplied. Arriving alone does not sell anything.” |
| 50–65 s | Match history; explain the merchant market or show a prepared cargo offer | “Players choose their own asking prices. Another merchant can buy a whole lot, then make the delivery.” |
| 65–80 s | Public match 000008 evidence and an NPC transaction link from chain-proof.md | “Online, NEAR enforces the economy. Tess submits her own moves from a separate wallet. Here are the recorded transaction proofs.” |
| 80–90 s | Return to the game and display the play/repository links | “Play solo to learn, or invite a friend. Can you finish richest and keep the lights on?” |

The supplied screenshots show **solo practice**, not newly signed online moves. Label recorded chain evidence as the completed testnet match. For live online footage, use two of your own testnet wallets and the judge walkthrough; account setup and transaction approvals belong outside a short recording where possible. Never expose private keys, recovery phrases, service credentials or personal contact details.
